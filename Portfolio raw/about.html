Hello, I'm Jada.

UX researcher and interaction designer, studying Human-Computer Interaction Design across Finland and the Netherlands.

A little about me

I'm an MSc Human-Computer Interaction Design student at the University of Twente and Aalto University. My background in Psychology and Technology grounds my work in how people think, behave, and interact with technology.

I specialize in qualitative UX research, usability testing, and spatial and interactive design. I connect research findings to practical design decisions, and enjoy making complex systems easier to understand and use.

What I'm working on

My double-degree master's focuses on UI/UX research, interactive technologies, VR/XR, motion UI, and cross-device interaction. I’m especially interested in how culture, perception, and persuasion shape human-technology interactions.

Outside of work

As a performing artist, I create stage motion graphics, promotional posters, and visual identities for live performances. I’ve also worked as secretary and social media manager for JCES Kinjin, supporting a community of 100+ members.

How I work

Human-centred research

I plan and conduct interviews, usability tests, co-design workshops, and eye-tracking studies, then translate patterns into actionable insights.

From insight to interaction

I connect psychology and HCI with journey maps, prototypes, and interaction design for digital, spatial, and physical experiences.

Visual and technical craft

I work across Figma, motion and visual design, physical prototyping, and web technologies to make and communicate ideas.
